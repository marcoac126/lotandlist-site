How the math works

Profit is the sale price, plus any shipping the buyer paid you, minus the platform fee, the label you paid for, packaging, and what you paid for the item. Return on cost is profit divided by what you paid.

Poshmark and local Facebook pickups ignore the shipping boxes, because on Poshmark the buyer pays for the label and local pickups have no label at all.

Why the cheapest fee isn't always the best platform

Lower fees help, but where your item actually sells, and for how much, matters more. Use this to see what each platform would leave you at the same price. If you need a specific profit, the list price calculator works backward from it.

Fee estimates are for US standard seller accounts, checked Oct 1, 2026 using the Underpriced reseller fee tracker. Ads, promotions, boosts, and international fees aren't included. eBay and Etsy also charge on the sales tax the buyer pays, and some eBay categories use other rates (handbags 15%, books 15.3%, sneakers $150+ 8%). Platforms change fees, so confirm on each platform's own fee page.
