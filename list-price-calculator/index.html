How it works

"Lowest price" is the least you can accept and still make your profit. "List at" adds your offer room. For each platform, the calculator finds the lowest price, to the cent, where your profit reaches your target after that platform's fee. It then adds your offer room and rounds up to a whole dollar, so you can accept offers and still hit your number.

"Shipping the buyer pays you" covers listings where the buyer pays shipping on top of the price. For free-shipping listings, leave it at 0 and put the label cost in "Shipping label you pay".

Picking your offer room

Poshmark and Mercari buyers expect to make offers, so 15–20% room is common there. On eBay with Buy It Now and no offers, you can set it to 0.

Fee estimates are for US standard seller accounts, checked Oct 1, 2026 using the Underpriced reseller fee tracker. Ads, promotions, boosts, and international fees aren't included. eBay and Etsy also charge on the sales tax the buyer pays, and some eBay categories use other rates (handbags 15%, books 15.3%, sneakers $150+ 8%). Platforms change fees, so confirm on each platform's own fee page.
