Why work backward from profit?

Most fee calculators tell you what's left after you pick a price. Resellers usually need the opposite: the price that still leaves the profit you want once fees, shipping, and offers take their cut. The list price calculator does that for every platform at once.

Why split a lot?

When ten items came out of one $40 box, each one's cost is a share of that $40. Without splitting it, cheap items look like losses and good finds look like pure profit. The lot cost calculator gives each item a fair share you can copy into your records.

Fee estimates are for US standard seller accounts, checked Oct 1, 2026 using the Underpriced reseller fee tracker. Ads, promotions, boosts, and international fees aren't included. eBay and Etsy also charge on the sales tax the buyer pays, and some eBay categories use other rates (handbags 15%, books 15.3%, sneakers $150+ 8%). Platforms change fees, so confirm on each platform's own fee page.
